## Assessment
- This is NOT a proven finding. It is an unreproduced design observation:
  - There is no PoC, no fork test, and no recorded address or source path.
  - The actor is the protocol's own trusted role (investmentManager), not an arbitrary attacker. Impact
    requires a malicious or compromised manager. Most bounty programs treat that as out of scope (admin/
    centralization), and teams often document such roles.
  - The key open question is unanswered: can the manager publish repeatedly with no cooldown, walking NAV
    +/-5% per publish? And is there a mint/redeem path where a skewed NAV extracts value from depositors?
    Without that, the per-publish 5% bound is itself a mitigation.
  - The +/-5% band, threshold()=1, and the $1.85M figure date from 2026-08-22 and were not re-checked.
- Promote only if all of the following hold: (1) the DVNPublisher address is found and threshold()==1 is read
  on-chain; (2) no publish cooldown or rate limit exists; (3) a fork PoC shows depositor value moving to the
  manager or its counterparties via NAV skew; (4) the team's docs do not already disclose manager NAV authority.

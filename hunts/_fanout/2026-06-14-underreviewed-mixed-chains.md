### Monolith Market  (DIG-CAREFUL)

  - lead: Lender.sol:549-612 / :654-656 (increaseDebt/decreaseDebt/getDebtOf) -- debt-share rounding asymmetry (the backlog flag): increaseDebt mints shares with mulDivUp and only re-checks against maxBorrowDeltaBps (tolerates up to ~2% inflation per borrow), while partial decreaseDebt burns shares with mulDivDown and getDebtOf reads debt back with mulDivUp. In a thin / freshly-seeded debt pool this asymmetry is the classic share-rounding inflation/grief surface (repeated dust borrows skew totalDebtShares/totalDebt; up-rounded readout vs down-rounded burn). Med if a borrower can dilute the pool's debt-share price or leave un-repayable dust against other borrowers; Low if maxBorrowDeltaBps + minDebt bound it to negligible dust.  [] (?)
